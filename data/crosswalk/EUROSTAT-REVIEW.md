# Eurostat crosswalk: the refusals

Two kinds, and they are not the same problem.

## Rejected as the wrong slice (18)

A head matched and every candidate's breakdown contradicted PORDATA's. These are the veto working: *Exports total and by type of energy product* is not *Exports by industry (FIGARO application)*. Worth reading for a case where the two vocabularies simply differ.

- `Fatal accidents at work total and by sex` — wanted *total and by sex*
- `General government expenditure by category (euro)` — wanted *by category*
- `General government expenditure by category (percentage)` — wanted *by category*
- `Agricultural holdings total and by dimension` — wanted *total and by dimension*
- `Exports total and by type of energy product` — wanted *total and by type of energy product*
- `Imports total and by type of energy product` — wanted *total and by type of energy product*
- `Deaths by certain causes as a percentage of total deaths` — wanted *by certain causes as a percentage of total deaths*
- `Deaths by certain causes` — wanted *by certain causes*
- `Labour force total and by age group` — wanted *total and by age group*
- `Labour force total and by sex` — wanted *total and by sex*
- `Unemployment by length of unemployment (percentage)` — wanted *by length of unemployment*
- `Unemployment total and by length of unemployment` — wanted *total and by length of unemployment*
- `Unemployment total and by previous occupation` — wanted *total and by previous occupation*
- `Employment total and by occupation` — wanted *total and by occupation*
- `At risk of poverty rate after social transfers by level of education` — wanted *by level of education*
- `At risk of poverty rate after social transfers by household type with dependent children` — wanted *by household type with dependent children*
- `Temporary employees total and by sex` — wanted *total and by sex*
- `Gross value added total and by sector of economic activity (euro)` — wanted *total and by sector of economic activity*
